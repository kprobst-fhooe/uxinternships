# Internships

Welcome to my personal Internship Guide! 

Over the past few years, I have compiled this guide based on my own experiences in teaching and coaching students in Human-Computer Interaction and UX design. For those interested in applying for jobs in this domain, the guide aims to provide you with practical advice to help you navigate the internship (job) application.


## Application

**First off, I recommend writing lots of applications, min. 20!** \
**Be prepared that many companies will take time to reply, or will not reply at all to your application.** \
**If they haven't replied to your application after 3-4 weeks or so, don't hesitate to send them a gentle reminder.**

Within your application journey, I can furthermore recommend the following:

- start with creating a professional CV and portfolio that highlight your experience
- have all this represented in a [professional LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/10-must-know-linkedin-profile-tips-job-seekers-beginners-guide-rt6lf/), which not only helps you find companies in the job offers, but also companies finding YOU
